How to Download Your e-PAN Online

Find the official route for downloading an e-PAN, depending on where your PAN application or service was processed.

Home › PAN & Aadhaar › How to Download Your e-PAN Online

Find the official route for downloading an e-PAN, depending on where your PAN application or service was processed.

If you used Instant e-PAN

  • Open the Income Tax e-Filing portal and choose Instant e-PAN under Quick Links.
  • Choose Check Status / Download PAN and continue.
  • Enter your Aadhaar number and complete OTP verification on the official portal.
  • If the e-PAN has been generated, choose View or Download e-PAN.

If you applied through a service provider

  • Use the official UTIITSL or Protean service that handled your application or correction.
  • Check the provider’s current e-PAN download eligibility, authentication and fee conditions.
  • Use the email address or mobile number linked to your PAN records when required.

Before downloading

  • Check that you are on the genuine official domain.
  • Do not share your Aadhaar OTP or PAN details with an unknown caller or website.
  • Save the PDF in a secure folder and avoid posting it publicly.
Important: The available download route depends on the application channel and eligibility. Follow the latest official portal instructions; fees and time windows may differ by provider.
