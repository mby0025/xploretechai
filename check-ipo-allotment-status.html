How to Check IPO Allotment Status

Learn where to look for an IPO allotment result and what to check if your application is not found.

Home › IPO & Investments › How to Check IPO Allotment Status

Learn where to look for an IPO allotment result and what to check if your application is not found.

Keep your application details ready

  • Keep your PAN, application number or bid details and demat information available.
  • Use only the registrar, stock exchange or broker links identified in the IPO’s official issue documents.

Check the result

  • Open the registrar’s official IPO allotment page or the relevant exchange service when the result is available.
  • Select the correct issue and enter the requested details exactly as shown in your application.
  • Submit the search and review the allotment quantity or status.
  • You can also check the application status through your broker or bank where that facility is provided.

If no record appears

  • Confirm that you selected the correct IPO and entered the right application details.
  • Check whether the basis of allotment has been finalised and results published.
  • Contact your broker, bank or registrar through the official contact details in the offer documents if the issue remains unresolved.

Check your bank account

  • If shares are allotted, verify the debit for the allotted amount.
  • If no shares are allotted or only part of the bid is allotted, check release of the remaining blocked amount.
Important: Allotment publication dates and service links vary by issue. Do not enter PAN or application details on unofficial websites or links received in unsolicited messages.
