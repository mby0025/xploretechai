Useful WhatsApp Privacy and Security Settings

Review important WhatsApp settings to control who can see your information and help secure your account.

Home › Mobile Tips & Apps › Useful WhatsApp Privacy and Security Settings

Review important WhatsApp settings to control who can see your information and help secure your account.

Review privacy settings

  • Open WhatsApp and go to Settings > Privacy. Menu names may differ slightly by device and app version.
  • Review Last Seen and Online, Profile Photo, About, Status and Groups. Choose the audience you are comfortable with.
  • Review read receipts and live location sharing. Turn off sharing you no longer need.

Secure your account

  • Enable two-step verification in Account settings and add a recovery email if offered.
  • Never share the six-digit registration code or two-step verification PIN.
  • Set a device screen lock and keep WhatsApp updated from the official app store.
  • Review Linked Devices and log out of devices you do not recognise.

Control chats and calls

  • Silence or block unwanted contacts and report suspicious messages using in-app options.
  • Be cautious with unknown links, files, QR codes and requests for money.
  • Check group invitation settings so unknown people cannot freely add you if you prefer tighter control.

If your account is compromised

  • Try to register your phone number again in the official app and follow the verification process.
  • Warn contacts not to send money or share codes in response to unusual messages from your account.
  • Use WhatsApp’s official Help Centre for account recovery guidance.
