How to Check a UPI Transaction Status

Find a UPI payment in your app, understand its status and raise a complaint using the transaction details.

Home › Banking & UPI › How to Check a UPI Transaction Status

Find a UPI payment in your app, understand its status and raise a complaint using the transaction details.

Find the transaction

  • Open the UPI app or bank app you used to make the payment.
  • Go to History, Transactions or Activity. Select the payment in question.
  • Note the date, amount, recipient and UPI transaction ID or reference number. Never publish or share sensitive account details publicly.

Understand common statuses

  • Successful: the app reports that the payment was completed. Check with the recipient if they have not received it.
  • Pending: the payment is still being processed or confirmation has not reached the app. Avoid paying again until you check the final status.
  • Failed: the payment did not complete. If money was debited, check the bank statement and the app’s reversal or refund status.

Raise a complaint

  • Open the transaction details and use the in-app Help, Support or Raise Complaint option.
  • Choose the closest issue, such as pending, failed or beneficiary not credited.
  • Save the complaint or ticket number and check its status in the app.
  • If unresolved, escalate through your bank’s official grievance process and the applicable NPCI or RBI complaint channel.

Keep records

  • Keep the transaction reference, date, amount, screenshots and complaint number.
  • Do not share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone claiming to investigate the payment.
Important: Resolution time can vary by transaction type, bank and network. Do not rely on a generic refund timeline; check the current status with your bank. XploreTechAI is an independent information website. It is not a government authority, bank, broker or investment adviser.
