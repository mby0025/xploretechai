  • OTP not received: Check network coverage, registered mobile details and message blocking; retry after a short interval.

  • Request pending: Track it with the acknowledgement/reference number and contact the official helpdesk if it exceeds the stated timeline.

5. Safety and privacy tips

  • Do not pay agents who promise guaranteed approval or faster processing.
  • Use HTTPS official domains and verify the exact web address.
  • Sign out after using a shared device and do not save passwords on public computers.
