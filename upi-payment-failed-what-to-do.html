What to Do When a UPI Payment Fails

Use these steps when a UPI payment fails, remains pending or money is debited without confirmation.

Home › Banking & UPI › What to Do When a UPI Payment Fails

Use these steps when a UPI payment fails, remains pending or money is debited without confirmation.

First, do not pay again immediately

  • Open the app’s transaction history and check whether the status is Failed, Pending or Successful.
  • If the status is Pending, wait for the app or bank to update it before retrying. A second payment could create a duplicate debit.

If money was debited

  • Check your bank statement, not only the notification screen.
  • Open the transaction and raise a complaint through the UPI app or bank app.
  • Record the UPI reference number, amount, date and complaint number.
  • Check the bank account for a reversal. If it does not arrive within the applicable timeline, contact your bank and escalate through its grievance process.

If the recipient says they did not receive it

  • Share only the transaction reference and status needed to identify the payment. Do not share PINs or OTPs.
  • Ask the recipient to check their bank statement and transaction history.
  • Use the app’s complaint option if the status and recipient’s records do not match.

If you suspect fraud

  • Immediately contact your bank through an official channel and ask it to secure the account or payment method.
  • Call India’s cyber-fraud helpline 1930 promptly and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • Change compromised credentials using the official app or website.
Important: The exact reversal and complaint timelines depend on the transaction type and applicable rules. Confirm the latest timeline with your bank or NPCI. XploreTechAI is an independent information website. It is not a government authority, bank, broker or investment adviser.
