Understanding ASBA and UPI Mandates for IPOs

Understand how IPO application money is blocked through ASBA or a UPI mandate and when it may be debited or released.

Home › IPO & Investments › Understanding ASBA and UPI Mandates for IPOs

Understand how IPO application money is blocked through ASBA or a UPI mandate and when it may be debited or released.

What is ASBA?

  • ASBA means Application Supported by Blocked Amount.
  • The application amount is blocked in your bank account rather than transferred to the issuer immediately.
  • If shares are allotted, the amount required for the allotment is debited. The balance is unblocked.

How the UPI mandate works

  • Submit an IPO application through an eligible intermediary and provide your own eligible UPI ID.
  • A mandate request is sent to your UPI app by the sponsor bank.
  • Check the issue, applicant and amount before approving.
  • Approve only through your own UPI app. Enter your UPI PIN only in the genuine app when you intend to authorise the block.

After allotment

  • The amount for allotted shares is debited according to the authorisation and issue process.
  • Any excess blocked amount is released according to the applicable process.
  • If there is no allotment, the blocked amount should be released; check your bank account and mandate status.

Important checks

  • Use the UPI apps and bank accounts permitted for the issue under current SEBI instructions.
  • Do not approve a mandate you did not initiate.
  • Do not share your UPI PIN or OTP with an intermediary, caller or message sender.
