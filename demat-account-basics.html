Demat Account Basics for Beginners

Learn what a demat account does, how it connects to a trading account and what to check before opening one.

What is a demat account?

  • A dematerialised account holds eligible securities in electronic form.
  • It is commonly used to hold shares and other securities credited to an investor.
  • A demat account is different from a bank account and a trading account.

Demat, trading and bank accounts

  • Bank account: holds money and is used for payments and receipts.
  • Trading account: used to place buy and sell orders through a broker.
  • Demat account: holds securities in electronic form through a depository participant.

Before opening an account

  • Compare account maintenance charges, transaction charges and other fees in the current tariff sheet.
  • Check the broker or depository participant’s registration and official contact details.
  • Understand nominee, account access, statements, pledging and account closure procedures.
  • Use strong authentication and keep contact details updated.

Common terms

  • DP: Depository Participant, the service provider through which you access depository services.
  • NSDL and CDSL: the two securities depositories in India.
  • ISIN: a unique identification code for a security.
  • CAS: Consolidated Account Statement, which can combine holdings across eligible accounts.
Important: Charges and account features differ between providers. Read the current terms and verify registration before opening an account.
