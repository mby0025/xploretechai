What Is Artificial Intelligence? A Simple Explanation

Learn what AI means, how it works in everyday apps and where you may already be using it.

Read this guide

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that helps computers perform tasks that usually require human-like abilities, such as understanding language, recognising images, finding patterns and generating content.

Everyday examples:

  • Maps suggesting a faster route.
  • Email apps filtering spam.
  • Voice assistants understanding spoken requests.
  • Chatbots answering questions and helping draft text.

AI can make mistakes, so verify important information before acting on it.

How to Use ChatGPT for Everyday Tasks

Explore practical ways to use an AI assistant for writing, learning, planning and understanding information.

Read this guide

ChatGPT can help you brainstorm ideas, explain difficult topics, draft messages, summarise text you provide and organise plans.

Try a clear prompt: “Explain how to check my PF passbook in simple steps. Include the official website and documents I may need.”

  • Tell it your goal and relevant context.
  • Ask for a step-by-step answer.
  • Request simpler wording if needed.
  • Check important facts against official sources.

Do not enter passwords, OTPs, bank PINs or other confidential details.

Digital safety

How to Identify Fake Links, Messages & Online Scams

Learn warning signs before clicking links or sharing personal information.

Read this guide

Be cautious of urgent messages asking you to claim a reward, avoid account closure or share an OTP.

  • Check the full website address before signing in.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords, UPI PINs or card PINs.
  • Do not install apps sent by unknown people.
  • Contact your bank or service provider through its official app or website.

If you suspect financial cyber fraud in India, contact your bank immediately and use the official cybercrime reporting channels.
