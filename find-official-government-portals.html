How to Find the Correct Government Service Portal

A checklist for finding genuine Indian government websites and avoiding fake portals or agents.

Home › Government Services › How to Find the Correct Government Service Portal

A checklist for finding genuine Indian government websites and avoiding fake portals or agents.

Start from a trusted source

  • Use the relevant ministry, department or official state government website to locate a service.
  • A .gov.in or .nic.in domain is a useful clue, but still check the exact spelling and page context.
  • Avoid sponsored search results or unsolicited messages that imitate government services.

Check the service details

  • Confirm that the page describes the exact service and state or department you need.
  • Read eligibility, document requirements, fees, processing time and grievance options before applying.
  • Check whether the service is available online or requires a visit to an authorised centre.

Submit information safely

  • Enter personal details only on the official portal after checking the domain.
  • Pay fees only through the payment route shown on the official portal and keep the receipt.
  • Never share OTPs or passwords with agents or callers.

Keep proof

  • Save the acknowledgement or application number.
  • Keep a copy of payment receipts and submitted documents in a secure location.
  • Use the portal’s official tracking and grievance facilities for follow-up.
Important: Government services differ by state and department. This guide is a navigation checklist, not a substitute for the department’s current rules. XploreTechAI is an independent information website. It is not a government authority, bank, broker or investment adviser.
