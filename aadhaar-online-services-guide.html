Aadhaar Online Services: A Beginner’s Guide

Find official Aadhaar services for downloading, checking status and managing selected Aadhaar details.

Home › PAN & Aadhaar › Aadhaar Online Services: A Beginner’s Guide

Find official Aadhaar services for downloading, checking status and managing selected Aadhaar details.

Use the official UIDAI website

  • Start at uidai.gov.in and choose the service you need.
  • Check whether the service requires an Aadhaar number, registered mobile number or OTP.
  • Use only the official UIDAI portal or the official mAadhaar app from its verified app-store listing.

Common online services

  • Download e-Aadhaar where eligible.
  • Retrieve or check Aadhaar-related information using the options provided by UIDAI.
  • Check the status of an update request using its acknowledgement or update request number.
  • Review available authentication and update services before visiting a centre.

Protect your Aadhaar information

  • Never share an OTP with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.
  • Use masked Aadhaar when a full Aadhaar number is not required and the service accepts it.
  • Do not post Aadhaar documents or QR codes on public websites or social media.

If your mobile number is not registered

  • Some online services require OTP verification to the registered mobile number.
  • Check UIDAI’s current instructions for services that require an in-person visit or authorised centre.
Important: Online service availability and update rules can change. UIDAI may require in-person verification for some changes. Confirm the current instructions before visiting a centre.
