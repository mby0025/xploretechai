How to Protect Your Online Accounts

Use stronger passwords, two-step verification and recovery settings to reduce the risk of account takeover.

Home › Online Safety › How to Protect Your Online Accounts

Use stronger passwords, two-step verification and recovery settings to reduce the risk of account takeover.

Use strong, unique passwords

  • Use a different password for every important account.
  • A reputable password manager can help create and store long, unique passwords.
  • Avoid passwords based on your name, birthday, phone number or common words.

Enable two-step verification

  • Turn on two-step verification for email, banking, social media and other important accounts where available.
  • Prefer an authenticator app, passkey or security key where supported.
  • Never approve a sign-in request you did not initiate.

Secure recovery options

  • Keep your recovery email and phone number current.
  • Review signed-in devices and recent security activity.
  • Remove access for old devices and apps you no longer use.

Recognise phishing

  • Check the full sender address and website domain before signing in.
  • Do not open unexpected attachments or enter credentials through links in urgent messages.
  • Go to the official website or app yourself instead of using a message link.
Important: No single setting guarantees safety. Review the security guidance provided by each service and act quickly if you suspect account access by someone else. XploreTechAI is an independent information website. It is not a government authority, bank, broker or investment adviser.
