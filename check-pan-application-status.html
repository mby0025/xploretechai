How to Check Your PAN Application Status

Track a PAN application or correction using the acknowledgement details from the official service provider.

Home › PAN & Aadhaar › How to Check Your PAN Application Status

Track a PAN application or correction using the acknowledgement details from the official service provider.

Identify where you applied

  • Check your acknowledgement receipt or confirmation email to identify whether the application was submitted through Protean or UTIITSL.
  • Keep the acknowledgement number, coupon number or other reference shown on your receipt.

Track a Protean application

  • Open the official Protean PAN service website.
  • Choose the relevant PAN application status service and enter the requested acknowledgement details.
  • Follow the on-screen instructions and note the displayed status.

Track a UTIITSL application

  • Open the official UTIITSL PAN status page.
  • Choose the application type and enter the coupon number generated after submission.
  • Submit the details and review the status.

If you cannot find the application

  • Check for typing errors and confirm the correct provider.
  • Allow for the processing period stated by the provider.
  • Contact the provider through its official helpdesk if the status remains unavailable.
Important: Use the provider named on your receipt. Status pages and reference fields may change; never enter PAN or identity details on an unofficial tracking site. XploreTechAI is an independent information website. It is not a government authority, bank, broker or investment adviser.
