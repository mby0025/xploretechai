How to Apply for an IPO in India

Understand the usual IPO application steps, ASBA and UPI mandate process before placing an application.

Home › IPO & Investments › How to Apply for an IPO in India

Understand the usual IPO application steps, ASBA and UPI mandate process before placing an application.

Before applying

  • Read the company’s offer document, risk factors, financial information and issue details.
  • Decide whether the investment suits your financial situation and risk tolerance. An IPO can fall in value after listing.
  • Use a valid demat account, eligible bank account and an application channel authorised for the issue.

Apply through your broker or bank

  • Sign in to your broker’s official app or use an eligible bank’s ASBA facility.
  • Select the IPO and enter the requested bid quantity and price within the issue rules.
  • Provide your own bank account details or eligible UPI ID as supported by the application route.
  • Review every detail before submitting.

Approve the mandate

  • For UPI applications, check that the mandate request matches the IPO, applicant and amount.
  • Approve only a genuine mandate in your own UPI app. Never share your UPI PIN.
  • The application amount is blocked; it is generally debited only to the extent of allotment.

After applying

  • Save the application number and check the application status with the broker, bank or exchange/registrar as applicable.
  • Check allotment through the registrar or exchange when the basis of allotment is published.
  • If no shares are allotted, check that the blocked amount is released. Contact your bank or intermediary if it is not.
Important: IPO processes, eligibility, UPI limits and deadlines can change. Check the current issue documents and SEBI instructions before applying. This guide is educational, not investment advice.
