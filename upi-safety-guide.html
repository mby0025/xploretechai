Set Up and Use UPI Safely in India

A practical checklist for setting up UPI, protecting your UPI PIN and checking payment requests before you approve them.

Home › Banking & UPI › Set Up and Use UPI Safely in India

A practical checklist for setting up UPI, protecting your UPI PIN and checking payment requests before you approve them.

Before you begin

  • Use the official app from Google Play or the Apple App Store. Check the developer name before installing.
  • Use the mobile number registered with your bank and keep your SIM active.
  • Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, debit-card PIN or screen-sharing access with another person.

Set up UPI

  • Open your bank’s or a supported UPI app and choose to add a bank account.
  • Select your bank. The app may verify the registered mobile number by SMS.
  • Choose the bank account you want to link and follow the app’s verification steps.
  • Set or create a UPI PIN only inside the genuine app, following the bank’s process. Do not tell the PIN to anyone.

Before approving a payment

  • Read the recipient name, amount and purpose carefully.
  • Remember: entering a UPI PIN generally authorises a debit or payment. You do not need to enter a PIN to receive money.
  • Do not scan a QR code or approve a collect request just because someone says it is needed to receive a refund or prize.

If something looks wrong

  • Stop the transaction. Do not share OTPs or install remote-access apps at another person’s request.
  • Contact your bank using the number on its official website or the back of your card.
  • For suspected financial cyber fraud in India, contact 1930 promptly and report at cybercrime.gov.in.
