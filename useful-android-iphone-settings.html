Useful Android and iPhone Settings to Review

A practical checklist for improving phone security, privacy, backups and everyday usability.

Home › Mobile Tips & Apps › Useful Android and iPhone Settings to Review

A practical checklist for improving phone security, privacy, backups and everyday usability.

Security basics

  • Use a strong screen lock and biometric unlock if suitable.
  • Install operating-system and app updates from official sources.
  • Turn on device-finding and recovery features and keep your account recovery details current.

Privacy checks

  • Review app permissions for location, camera, microphone, photos and contacts.
  • Remove permissions that an app does not need for its main function.
  • Review location sharing and emergency contacts.

Backup and storage

  • Enable a backup method you understand and check when it last completed successfully.
  • Make sure you know which account stores your photos and files.
  • Review large downloads and app storage before deleting anything.

Avoid common risks

  • Install apps only from official stores or trusted sources.
  • Do not install unknown configuration profiles, APKs or remote-access apps at a stranger’s request.
  • Never share OTPs, passwords or banking PINs.
